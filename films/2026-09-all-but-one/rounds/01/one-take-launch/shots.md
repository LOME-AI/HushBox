# One-take launch: shot table

Round 1 take, genre: an Apple-keynote launch film, 2D, one continuous take. Nothing cuts or fades: every picture is made of the one before it, and springs settle every arrival.

The take tries: the Devil hosts a keynote for the AI he loves, the product he presents becomes an eye that watches, the eye cracks and its secrets fly out as demons, the demons build his laughing face, he tries to swallow one last product and cannot, and that product (a Signal Red box) turns out to be HushBox, whose values he lists in terror before it unfolds into the HushBox mark.

Grid: 120 BPM, 30 frames a beat at 60 fps, 60 beats (15 bars of 4/4), 30.0 s. D minor keynote groove, a riser into a silent half beat, a driven drop on the leak, silence under "All…", a braam slam on "…but one", a trembling half-time under the values, D major on the reveal.

Nothing was kept from an earlier round: this is round 1. After the first film-reviewer pass (review.md, review 1), shots 1, 2, 5, 6 and 8 to 12 were restaged as the rows below give them, and every impact envelope now ramps in over the frame and a half before its hit, so motion blur draws no double exposure and each hit's own frame already moves. After review 2, shots 1, 2, 8, 10, 11 and 12 were restaged again as the rows below give them, and the value field's grade now travels: white, the Devil's ink, white, warm charcoal from the code beat, a white spotlight on the plea, white, then the charcoal close. After review 3, act one was retimed so "It keeps every word." holds 1.5 s over the growing stack before the pull-out on b11.

## Colour

Signal Red (read from the brand through `ctx.brand`) marks only the HushBox box, its lock, its shock ring and zaps, and the mark. The Devil is brimstone orange, the AI world electric blue, the demons ink. The grade travels: stage black, blue eye, white leak, orange face, violet-black mouth, white values, warm charcoal close.
