# All But One: Origin flight (round 1)

**Genre and reference.** "Origin", the founder's reference film: one continuous transformation in which every picture is made out of the last, with camera flights across scale (exponential zooms of 20 to 40 times) that hand one world to the next at a tinted flash. This take tells the brief's story in that vocabulary. There is no hard cut in it: every world is found inside a point of the last one, or the last one shrinks to a point inside the next.

**The idea in one line.** The Devil's world is made of kept, watched and leaked light; the one spiral he cannot take is the HushBox mark, and it swallows him.

**The rhyme the take rests on.** The HushBox mark (`packages/ui/src/assets/HushBoxLogo.png`) is itself a spiral galaxy: a centre dot and seven swept arcs. The Devil's galaxy of kept words at 4.5 s is its gold, greedy mirror; the field of "every company" at 14 s is hundreds of little gold spirals; the one spiral that stays red is the mark, and the film ends on it. The mark is drawn from code as geometry measured off the logo file (`mark.ts`: seven tapered stroke centrelines and the dot, fitted to the PNG's pixels at an intersection over union of 0.93), so it can be flown into at any scale.

## The end card's mark

The look host hands a look only fonts and brand colours (`films/engine/look/contract.ts`, `LookContext`); it offers no path for an image, and the frame function is synchronous, so the logo file cannot be loaded into a frame purely. The end card therefore draws the fitted geometry in `mark.ts`, like the flights. Using the real file needs an asset path in the host.

## Beat grid and music

- **Grid.** 120 BPM at 60 fps: a beat is 30 frames (0.5 s), a bar is 2 s, 60 beats in 30.0 s. A new composition lands every one to two seconds; every flight hand-off, burst, strike and value hit is on a beat or a half beat, and its sound is on the same frame (cue list in `score.ts`).
- **Music** (engine instruments only; D minor, no voice). A heartbeat and a low drone under the ember; a reverse swell into the burst; the Devil's charm as an FM-bell arpeggio in eighths that doubles to sixteenths in the galaxy (each bell note lights one word, Origin's arpeggio twinkle); a sly rotary-organ riff and a half-time pulse under his first lines; a crack, a riser and a doubling snare roll into a silent half-beat inhale; the drop at 10.0 s (impact, braam, glass, snap) into a driven half-time trap groove with supersaw stabs, a shriek per demon and a tom-and-crack per city strike; his laugh as climbing staccato stabs and a fire roar; silence; "All but one." on the heaviest blow; a racing heartbeat and a trembling pad; then one chord per HushBox value (B♭, C, Dm, A) on four-on-the-floor kicks, each value an impact and a braam; a downlifter and rumble through the collapse, a last silence, and D major on the mark.

## Shots

**Register: story.** Nine shots of 2 to 5 s. Frames are 60 fps; beats are 0.5 s. "Hand-off" means the old world's camera zooms exponentially into (or out of) a point while the new world grows out of that point, the seam sealed by a short flash tinted toward the arriving world. Inside a shot a caption enters at least half a beat after the action it names, so one read starts at a time; captions spring up into place with a small overshoot and lift away as they leave.

**What changed in the second revision, and why.**
- **Captions** now show through a slot: each line rises out of the slot's lower edge on a spring that overshoots, and lifts out through its upper edge. Blocks centre on the frame's centre line; a line too wide for that moves left only as far as the safe box needs.
- **Signal Red** is on the mark and its own glow only. The act's ground is cold graphite with ash, the shell's hits are white, the red rays are gone, and the end card's dust is warm grey.
- **Hits on their frames:** the gather snaps on 570, the collapse lurches and swells on 1560, the arcs snap into the dot on 1650, and each city strike has a white impact star on its own frame, with older rings let go.
- **Holds:** "No. Not that one.", "And leak it all." and the two value blocks hold 1.5 s or more, with no strike in flight while they are up. To fit, the values now come in two blocks after two strikes.
- **The end card** lands with overshoot, then has events after 1683: the tagline one line at a time, a click on the second bell, a pulse on the third, and a real push.
- **Twins:** the eyes open apart, the wink is at 210 with one brow high, the talons curl shut one after another as the arm stretches thin, and the three demons are three figures.
- **Artefacts:** the bloom read coarse mip levels bilinearly and printed their blocks as a square in the pupil, a square core at the burst and a clipped rectangle behind the Devil. It now reads each level through a tent filter.

## Colour and light arc

Amber ember on black → hot white burst → gold Devil → violet galaxy of gold words → cyan eye → violet cracks and a violet-white burst → acid green, violet and cyan demons → a blue night planet with gold cities → gold field → one Signal Red mark → the red mark on cold graphite → warm charcoal with Signal Red. Every hand-off is sealed by a short flash tinted toward the arriving world, kept low so the flights, not whiteouts, carry the change; only the burst and the drop flash hard. The worlds alternate light and dark so each flight ends somewhere visibly new: amber-lit Devil, dark violet galaxy, pale grey eye, black-violet demons, deep blue planet, gold field, graphite ground under the red mark, charcoal card. Signal Red appears only on the HushBox mark and its light.

## What is drawn here

Everything: the dust, the burst, the Devil's face (a rig of horns, almond eyes with slit pupils that round in fear, brows, a crescent mouth with triangle teeth, goatee, ears, rising embers, every part a particle on a closed-form target that moves with his expression springs), the galaxy and its word-bubble sprites, the eye (lids, sclera veins, iris fibres, the surveillance dial, cracks), the demons (bat wings with finger bones and scalloped membranes of word bubbles, horns, claws, tails), the planet (land, clouds, city lights, rim light, atmosphere), the spiral field, the claw and its burn, the mark as particles and as geometry, the shell and its hits, the cipher sprites, the type's word-by-word entrance, and the finish (bloom, tinted flashes, aberration on impacts, vignette, grain, the text scrim).
