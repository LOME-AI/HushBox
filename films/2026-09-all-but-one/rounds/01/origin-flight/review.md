STAGING: HOLDS. The story order works and has its own look (the devil tells it, the HushBox mark wins, the end card closes it). The failures are in how it is timed and drawn: cues land late, captions arrive on top of the action, one crowd moves in unison, some poses hold too long, and four crops are smeared.

ITEMS:
- The composition changes at least every 2 s — PASS — On the sheet no two cells 2 s apart show the same composition. The longest near-hold is the devil face, 135 to 225, and even there the caption and grin change.

After that: grin dive at 270, word galaxy at 300, eye at 405, crack at 540, burst at 600, demons at 645, world at 735, gold field at 840, red mark at 960, burn at 1020, dive to red at 1110, then pushes at 1200, 1290, 1380 and 1470, collapse at 1560, end card at 1680.

The galaxy shot (285 to 390, 1.75 s), the four push shots (1200, 1290, 1380, 1470, about 1.5 s each) and "Nothing I can use." (1575 to 1665) are cut at hype length. Meanwhile the eye shot (405 to 600, 3.25 s) is too long for hype, so the shots fit neither register.
- Framing varies: wide, medium, close, extreme close — PASS — Wide: the world at 735 and the gold field at 870. Medium: the devil at 120. Close: the eye at 420. Extreme close: the grin dive at 255 and the mark at 1230.
- Every shot has a camera move and an event — FAIL — "No. Not that one." (1140 to 1185) is a locked frame. The strip 1650-last-breath shows the mark turning with the framing identical from 1647 to 1653. The end card (1695 to 1785) has no camera move at all.
- Every shot ends in a state it did not begin in — PASS — Each shot ends in a new state, for example the eye goes from plain (405) to pierced and flashed (600), and the world from clean (735) to marked (825). The four push shots do cycle back to the same arrangement (mark on top, devil beaming from below), which the travel figure reflects.
- One read at a time, each held long enough to land — FAIL — Strip 0750-strike-1: the green strike lands (751) while "world" is still revealing. Strip 1200-v1: the beam hit, the push-in and "Encrypted" all start between 1200 and 1203. Strip 1470-v4: the push and "No" arrive together. - Arcs, follow-through, squash and stretch on impact, strong key poses — FAIL — The arcs pass: the comet trails at 750 to 810 and the demon tails show follow-through. The impacts do not squash: in strip 0780-strike-2 the comet turns into a ring with no deformation. In strip 1200-v1 the mark does not give when the beam hits it at 1200.
- Type enters and leaves with motion, and holds long enough to read — FAIL — Every caption appears word by word as an opacity or blur fade in place ("They" at 271 to 273, "And" at 481 to 483, "Every" at 631 to 633, "No." at 1111 to 1113). - Frame 0 is composed, readable and moving — PASS — frame-0000 shows "INTERVIEW: THE DEVIL" legibly above a glowing point, and the particle field brightens across strip 0000-open (0 to 3).
- Every point reads with the sound off, as text inside the safe area — PASS — Every beat has its own caption inside the frame and away from the edges. They stay legible over the mark (frame-1230, frame-1350, frame-1620), though some are brief, as noted above.
- Signal Red marks HushBox and nothing else — PASS — Red appears only on the mark, its ring (990) and its glow and rays (1140 to 1680). The lightning is violet and the demons are green and lilac.
- The piece has its own look — PASS — Everything is drawn in particles made for this piece: the devil mask, the eye, the demons, the planet, the gold spiral field. The only centred text on dark is the end card (1695 to 1785).
