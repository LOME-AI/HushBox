# All But One: brief

Agreed with the founder on 2026-09-29.

## The idea

The Devil, delighted by AI companies that keep, watch and leak everything, turns to fear at the one company he cannot use: HushBox.

## References

- "Origin", the founder's reference film: one point of light becomes a galaxy, a planet, a tree and a figure, each picture made out of the last (`docs/runs/2026-09-26-films-from-code/research/founder-reference.md`).
- The Western-civilization film by @IterIntellectus: rapid hard cuts on the beat, kinetic type (`docs/runs/2026-09-26-films-from-code/research/reference-animations.md`).

Round 1 makes three takes of different kinds, and one of them is built on "Origin".

## Explicit instructions

- No human voice: music and effects only.
- Sensational, with hard drops, emotion and a story.
- Not technical: convey the values people want to hear.
- Statements about AI companies are general and need no deep fact-checking. Every claim about HushBox stays true.

## Beats from the founder's idea

Open to change unless the founder fixes them:

- The Devil is interviewed and praises AI companies for keeping everyone's data, monitoring them for wrong opinions, and leaking it for the world to see.
- The deepest, darkest secrets escape into the world as demons.
- "All but one."
- The Devil lists HushBox's values, in fear.

## Length

40 s, one piece (the founder's instruction of 2026-09-30, replacing 30 s). A 15 s cutdown follows once the film is delivered.

## 2026-09-29, from the round-1 verdict

- Keep the beat-cut take's frame: the interview file HUD, the secrets-kept counter and the BPM readout. The founder's words: "i loved the frame around the best cut with the interview, secrets kept and bpm. keep that"
- The film is an interview: the interviewer's question is on screen, and the Devil answers in the first person, the way the founder's idea has him speak ("Do I like AI companies? Of course I do, what's there not to like?").
- The hook shows rather than tells: no narrator line stating the thesis ("The Devil loves AI." was too on the nose).
- A bit scarier, with the same energy: menace over cartoon, at the beat-cut take's tempo and hard drops.
- The leaked secrets read as real, personal messages (the one-take take's "I'm in love with my best friend.", "I lied about the money.").
- The HushBox mark matches the real logo exactly, and still animates.

## 2026-09-30, from the round-2 verdict

- No leaked message in the opening hook: the secrets' topic is not introduced yet ("remove the "im in love with my best friend" at the start, that is confusing").
- The leak is one-take-launch's scene from round 1: secrets escaping as a flock of demons carrying their lines, gathering into the Devil's laughing face.
- The move from the demons around his head back to the interview is eased, not a hard jump.
- The end screen sits near still for at least 5 seconds.

## 2026-09-30, from the second round-2 verdict

- 40 s, mostly by slowing everything down so the text can be read, with no other change from that.
- The move from the demons overhead into the "All of them?" scene flows, and the music carries through it rather than restarting.
- No grab of a light at the start: straight into the question and the answer.
- The demons circling the Devil's head either serve a purpose or go.
- The vault cracking open to reveal messages is replaced: the escaping demons already carry the secrets, so that scene follows one-take-launch's round-1 staging instead.
- The "All but one" scene is replaced: the search across many that ends on the one that turns out to be HushBox, as in round 1 (origin-flight's field of gold spirals, where the one lit red is the HushBox mark).

## 2026-09-30, from the verdict on the 40 s take

- The search for the one ("All but one") is a dramatic spectacle, not a quiet scan.
- The secrets-kept counter starts at 0 and rises from the first frame.
- The leak's crack gets a stronger visual element.
- The "I think I want a divorce." secret is replaced by one that is embarrassing and gives comic relief.
