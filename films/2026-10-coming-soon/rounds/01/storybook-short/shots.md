# Coming Soon: storybook short (round 01, after review 2)

An animated short in storybook line art, 31.2 s, led by her. It opens mid-deletion: the question she keeps deleting is being eaten from its end, and every deleted letter pops off the composer, turns into a white letter-board capital and falls onto a pile of letters already on the floor below her hands. She sits by the heap, worried, looks up at the blank letter board on the wall, reaches toward it, and smiles. She types the question again in full and sends it. The answers build her cinema in gold around her: a chart from code becomes the rows of seats; a web search's sources fall outside and lay the street; an image answer rises from her phone, sketches a marquee and becomes the sign; four models suggest names and she picks LANTERN. That night the conversation leaves her phone as pages and papers the glass doors, writing facing her; from the street the doors are blank paper with COMING SOON, then a small Signal Red spiral, then a taped note: "Not even we can read it." At dawn she peels the paper down, kneels at the heap, and spells OPEN on the board with four of the letters she deleted. She opens the door; its bell rings; the sign lights; she beams. The page turns to the end card.

## Grid and music

- 100 BPM: 36 frames a beat, 4/4, 52 beats = 1872 frames (31.2 s at 60 fps). Every shot cuts on a whole beat; arrivals and hits land on beats, half beats or quarter beats.
- F major, a small chanson: music box (FM bell), a squeeze-box (organ, musette rotor) on the off-beats, a plucked 808 bass, pads.
  - The doubt (0-11.5): D minor pads; a music-box note on every third letter that pops; a typewriter knock for every letter that lands on the pile (the first on frame 0); a lift to F with a sub as she looks up (5.5); typing ticks as she finishes the question (8); a riser into a half beat of silence (11, the inhale).
  - The answers (11.5-29): Send (11.5) is the pick-up; the oom-pah starts on 12; pops climb with the chart's bars; a tick as the search answer scrolls away (19.5); a tom for each row (14.5-15.25) and each slab (20.25-21.5), a snap with each slab; a match strike for the lamp (22); a whoosh as the image card rises (22.5) and as it morphs (25); a soft impact as the sign lands (25.5); a pop and a climbing music-box note for each name (26.5-27.25); a bell and chord as LANTERN lights (28.5).
  - Night (29-39): the groove stops; pads and a slow music box; a paper whoosh, snap and tape tick per page; outside, the street's room tone, a bell for the spiral (34.5), a snap and tick for the note (35.5), a whoosh for the gust (37.5); a riser into a half beat of silence as she grabs the paper (39).
  - Dawn (38-46): the paper comes down on a soft impact, a reverse swell and a full F major chord (39.5); a tick for each letter she picks (40.75-41.5); OPEN on four rising notes, C D E F (42.5-44); the shop bell as she opens the door (44.5); the oom-pah returns.

## Choices worth stating

- The film opens on the deletion because it is the one image every tested viewer remembered; the pile already on the floor at frame 0 says she has deleted it before.
- Every answer leaves the phone as a gold shape or card and becomes the thing it built in the world around her: the cinema is made of answers, the AI is her help, and she stays the protagonist.
- The privacy point is made by the note alone in the held shot, after the viewer has just watched the conversation paper the doors facing her.
