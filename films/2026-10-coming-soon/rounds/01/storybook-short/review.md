TAKE: films/2026-10-coming-soon/rounds/01/storybook-short/out/
VERDICT: FAIL
STAGING: FAILS — the shots themselves fail in only two places, the opening 2 s and the 576–701 run of short shots; every other failure is in how the shots are drawn and timed.
ITEMS:
- The composition changes at least every 2 s — PASS — Nothing holds longer than about 2 s. Two stretches sit at the limit. The note tableau runs 1206–1331 (about 2.1 s) and is carried only by a slow push-in. The end card is fully still for 1678–1799 (about 2.0 s).

The first striking image, the letters peeling off the composer, starts at sheet 195, which is 3.25 s (strips/0216-rain). After that, payoffs land at intervals of no more than 3.6 s through to the mark at 1638.
- Shot lengths suit a story piece, 2 to 5 s — FAIL — Two shots run short: the bars-to-seats shot (576–647) runs 1.2 s and the search shot (648–701) runs 0.9 s. Every other shot runs 2.4–4.5 s.
- Framing varies — PASS — Wide: stills/frame-0252 (the room) and strips/0774-lamp (the exterior). Medium: stills/frame-0972 and stills/frame-1476. Close: stills/frame-0000 and stills/frame-0576. Extreme close: stills/frame-0504 (the chart punch-in) and crops/0108-thumb-at-send.
- Every shot has a camera move and an event — FAIL — The end card (1638–1799) has events but no camera move: stills/frame-1692 and stills/frame-1764 are identical. Every other shot drifts, pushes or pulls.
- Every shot ends in a state it did not begin in — PASS — Composer to letters gone; crouched to looking up; empty composer to chart; bars to seats; header to source cards; dark doors to LANTERN lit; bare doors to pages; blank doors to COMING SOON and the note; dusk to dawn with the letters gathered; empty board to OPEN with the door open; dark to mark and tagline.
- One read at a time, each held long enough to land — FAIL — Search shot: "Gemini 3.1 Pro", "Searched the web · 6 sources" and a fan of cards are on screen for 649–701, about 0.85 s. Name cards: four two-line cards arrive about 15 frames apart (866–915) before the pick at 936. strips/1188-spiral: the red spiral (1189) and the note (1190) arrive one frame apart, two reads at once.
- Every pose moves on once read — FAIL — The thumb hovers motionless over send for about 105–165; she stands motionless looking up for about 1575–1634; the end card freezes for 1678–1799 and the mark stops turning.
- Arrivals carry overshoot or anticipation — FAIL — Bar 3 overshoots and the send has anticipation, but the wordmark and the tagline arrive by opacity alone, and the note eases straight to rest with no overshoot.
- Paired and repeated parts move differently — FAIL — The two glowing bars rotate in lockstep (591–597); two paving cards fall side by side at the same height and tilt (717–720); each seat row changes all at once (609–615).
- Arcs, follow-through, squash and stretch, key poses — FAIL — Arcs and key poses hold, but no impact squashes: the thumb on send stays rigid, the paving cards and the note land flat; nothing trails her movements; the bun pencil turns rigidly with her head.
- Type enters and leaves with motion and holds long enough to read — FAIL — The wordmark and tagline fade on in place; "Searched the web · 6 sources" holds about 0.85 s and leaves on the hard cut at 702.
- Every hit strip shows a visible action on the hit's frame — FAIL — No action on the hit frame: 0108-hover, 1620-page-turn. One or two frames late: 0774-lamp, 0864-names, 1188-spiral, 1656-wordmark, 1674-tagline, 0306-retry-delete. Early: 1548-letter-n.
- Every crop holds up at full size — FAIL — 0648-rows-and-her is an empty dark phone screen; 0612-her-turns shows only the back of her head; 1368-reach: face half outside the crop and crossed by her arm, page text clipped mid-word (also stills/frame-1044, crops/1080-pages-and-her); 0000-header-and-question shows no question; 0756-her-inside: her face is an eye dot with no mouth, so no expression reads.
- Frame 0 is composed, readable and moving — PASS — stills/frame-0000 shows the composer with "Is it crazy t" legible, the HushBox header, and the thumb mid-stroke.
- Every point reads with the sound off, as text inside the safe area — FAIL — After the punch-in the "GPT-5.5" label sits about 24 px from the left edge (stills/frame-0504) and is cut to ".5" during the bar build (555–561).
- Signal Red marks HushBox and nothing else — PASS — Red appears only on HushBox's own surfaces; the fifth source dot in stills/frame-0684 is salmon, not Signal Red.
- The piece has its own look — PASS — A flat, outlined storybook with effects made for this piece; only the end card falls back on centred text fading in on a dark gradient.
