# Coming Soon: one-take launch (round 01)

An Apple-keynote launch film in one continuous take, 30 s, 2D only. The camera is a multiplane dolly: every layer of the world (the street, the facade and its glass doors, her and the pile just inside, five rows of seats, the screen on the back wall) sits at its own depth, so pulling out through the glass and flying down the hall into the screen are one unbroken camera move with true parallax. Nothing fades, blurs or cuts: every scene is made out of the one before. The app's cards float over the world, keynote style, and each one hands its piece to the world by flying into it: the code's chart becomes the screen and the rows of seats, the search's source chips become the paving slabs, the image becomes the marquee, the picked name flies onto it, the conversation becomes the paper on the doors, and the screen it all started from becomes the end card.

The story, as the viewer reads it: at night, sitting on the floor of an empty room, she has just deleted something: its letters are still raining down into a pile beside her on frame 0. She types "is it crazy to quit my job", reaches for Send, pulls back, and deletes it word by word; the letters fall and land on the pile. She sits up, types "How do I open a cinema?" and sends it. The answers build the cinema round her: she leans in at the chart, stands as the seats land and turns to look at them, points as the street is laid, shades her eyes as the sign goes up, and picks the name with a tap, seen close. Her free hand acts on every beat of hers (it types, reaches, covers her mouth, pushes back her hair, cups her chin, pumps a fist, rests on her hip, points, scrolls) while the other keeps the phone. At night she holds her phone up and the conversation papers the glass doors, page by page, each page turning to face her; from the street the doors are blank paper with COMING SOON, a small Signal Red spiral and a taped note, "Not even we can read it." At dawn the paper comes down, she opens the doors, and spells OPEN on the letter board with letters from the pile, flinging each one up with a sweep of her arm; the camera dives onto the N as it lands, until the letter fills the frame, then pulls back out to the whole cinema; she cheers, seen close. The camera flies past her down the hall into the screen, which shows the HushBox mark: the end card.

The pile's letters are her deletions. The attempt still falling on frame 0 was "should i open a cinema", too scattered to read as it falls; the O, P, E and N she spells at dawn are its letters, so the word she was afraid to send is the word she hangs on the door.

Signal Red appears only on HushBox: the Send button, the spiral on the paper, the mark, and "Box" in the wordmark.

## Grid and music

- 120 BPM, 30 frames a beat, 4/4, 60 beats = 1800 frames (30 s at 60 fps). Every shot boundary is a whole beat; every arrival and hit lands on a beat or a half beat. From 16 to 28 one piece of the cinema is read at a time, each held at least 2.5 beats (1.25 s): the hall, the street, the image, the sign. Arrivals are pre-rolled: a card, a bar, a row, the road, the sign or a letter is already moving on its cue frame and lands with an overshoot or a squash, so every hit shows on its own frame.
- D major, music and effects only.
  - Night (0-11): a soft pad (Dmaj9, Bm9, Gmaj7) over room tone; from frame 0 a bell and a soft thud for each handful of letters landing on the pile, on a falling line; a key tick per typed character; a bell each time she hesitates (3.5, 4.5); each deleted group's lift a pop, its landing a bell on a falling line with a thud; a bell as she decides (8); a riser into Send.
  - The build (11-34): Send is the pickup; four on the floor from 12, claps on two and four, offbeat hats, an 808 line under D, Bm, G, A, D. A sound for every piece of the cinema on its frame: a rising bell per chart bar, a whoosh as the card flies, a thud and clack per row (lower as the rows come nearer), a tick and thud per four slabs, a tick per denoise step, an impact as the sign lands, a bulb arpeggio, a rising bell per name card, a bell on the pick, an impact, stab and chime as the name lights.
  - Night again (34-48): a soft kick on every beat under the paper's breathing light; a paper slap per page; an impact as COMING SOON is thrown (36) and a slap as each letter lands; HushBox's chime (a rising fifth, D5 to A5) and a sub on the spiral; a snap for the note and one for its tape; a passing car's whoosh peaking on 40; a reverse swell into dawn's chime on 42; a whoosh per sheet peeled; a bell per letter of OPEN on a rising line, each with a clack; a riser into half a beat of silence (47.5).
  - Open and the close (48-60): OPEN on the full groove with a swell, stab, impact and chime; the cheer's stab and bell; a whoosh and riser for the flight; an impact, sub, chime and the D chord as the screen fills; a bell on the wordmark and one on the tagline, the chord ringing under the card.
