# Review: one-take-launch

## 2026-10-03, review 1

TAKE: films/2026-10-coming-soon/rounds/01/one-take-launch/out/
VERDICT: FAIL
STAGING: FAILS — the chain of story beats holds (words fall into a pile, bars become seats, chips become floor tiles, an image becomes the marquee, pages cover the windows, the doors open, the camera flies into the screen). Three faults are in the shots themselves: the 2.5 s opening before anything striking, the 2.25 s static end card, and the seated pose held about 22 s. The other failures are in how the shots are drawn and timed.
ITEMS:
- The composition changes at least every 2 s — FAIL — the end card does not change from 1650 to 1785 (2.25 s). Two stretches come close: 0–105 (1.75 s, only the typing changes) and 1185–1290 (1.75 s, only a slow drift on "ING SOON" and the note).
- Something striking lands within the first 2 s, then a payoff every 3 to 5 s — FAIL — frames 0–149 are a composer being typed over a woman sitting in a dark room; the first striking event is "job" popping out of the composer at 150 (2.5 s). After that the payoffs come often enough: code card 390, bars into seats 507–513, chips into slabs 597, marquee 735, name lit 900, COMING SOON 1065, note 1123, peel 1290, OPEN 1365–1410, flight into the screen 1532–1590, wordmark 1620.
- Shot lengths suit the piece (story, 2 to 5 s) — FAIL — the opening (0–270, 4.5 s) and COMING SOON (1065–1290, 3.75 s) are in range; the search card 537–630 is about 1.55 s and the image card 657–735 about 1.3 s.
- Framing varies — PASS — wide street at frame-1500, medium room at frame-0510, close on her face at frame-0120, extreme close on the dot in the screen at frame-1560.
- Every shot has a camera move and an event — FAIL — the end card from 1650 to 1785 has neither. The close on COMING SOON (1185–1290) drifts, but no event shows at its labelled car and dawn cues (strips 1200-car and 1260-dawn).
- Every shot ends in a state it did not begin in — PASS — the composer becomes a pile of words, the dark room becomes the marquee street, the blank windows become COMING SOON then OPEN, the logo gains the wordmark and the tagline.
- One read at a time, each held long enough to land — FAIL — the four name cards land 0.25 s apart at 777, 792, 807 and 822, each a two-line read, stacking (crop 0810-name-cards). The code on the card is readable from 390 until it squashes before 420, under 0.5 s.
- Every pose moves on once read — FAIL — the seated pose holding the phone stays from frame 0 to about 1305 (about 21.75 s); only her face and the phone's glow change. The end card is frozen from 1650.
- Arrivals carry overshoot or anticipation — FAIL — the code card appears whole at 390; the wordmark cuts on at 1620 and the tagline at 1635; "E" (1392–1395) and "N" (1407–1410) drop into OPEN and stop dead. Only the note at 1123 lands with a settle.
- Paired and repeated parts move differently — FAIL — the cheer raises both arms into a matching V (frame-1500, frame-1530, crop 1500-cheer); the seat rows resolve out of the slab all at once at 510.
- Arcs, follow-through, squash and stretch, strong key poses — FAIL — words travel on arcs and key poses read, but the letters land rigid in the pile (177–183) and "N" lands rigid at 1410; her hair and sweater show no follow-through when she stands and cheers.
- Type enters and leaves with motion and holds long enough to read — FAIL — the wordmark and the tagline cut on whole, and the code is readable for under 0.5 s.
- Every hit strip shows a visible action on the hit's frame — FAIL — nothing on the hit frame: 0405-run, 0450-bar-3 (451), 0495-seats-morph (497), 0600-chips-3 (602), 0645-lamp, 0780-name-1 (already settled by 779), 1200-car, 1260-dawn, 1530-fly (1532). These land on their frame: pop-1, land-1, send, reply, rows, sign, letter-3, letter-4, wordmark, tagline.
- Every crop holds up at full size — PASS — her face at 0000 reads tired, the cheer at 1500 reads as a smile; code, chart, chips, name cards, pick check, note, pile and end card are crisp. Ten of 28 crops not opened.
- Frame 0 is composed, readable and moving — PASS — the composer sits across the middle with "is it cr|", she is in the lower third with the pile beside her, the next keystroke lands at frame 3.
- Every point reads with sound off, as text in the safe area — PASS — every line is inside the frame.
- Signal Red marks HushBox and nothing else — PASS — red on the HushBox send button, the window logo, the dot, the logo and "Box"; the search chip "A" is mauve.
- The piece has its own look — PASS — the effects are drawn for this piece; only the end card is centred type on dark.
NUMBERS: turnover 4 (lowest window, 0–10 s); travel 0.35 — travel between the founder's references (0.42 and 0.26), well above the rejected openings (0.12 and 0.05); turnover below the 5 to 8 of pieces with reach, every later window 5 to 7.
